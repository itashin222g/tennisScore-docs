Privacy Policy

App: Tennis Score Watch

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Overview

Tennis Score Watch is a Wear OS app for recording tennis scores, match history, and match setting presets. This privacy policy explains how the app handles data.

Data Collection

Tennis Score Watch does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Data Stored on Device

The app may store the following data locally on the user's device:

  • Current match score and match state
  • Match history
  • Saved match setting presets

This data is stored only on the device and is not sent to the developer or to any external server by the app.

Data Sharing

Tennis Score Watch does not share user data with third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on the user's device until the user clears it in the app, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app.

Permissions

The app may use vibration or haptic feedback to notify the user when points are added or actions are undone. This does not involve collecting or sharing personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the email address listed in the app's Google Play store listing.
